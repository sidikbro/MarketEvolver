# Geopolitical Intelligence Lab

## Uncertainty is state

A geopolitical record represents what was supportable at a local observation
cutoff, not an omniscient account of an event. Confirmation remains explicit as
unverified, partially confirmed, confirmed, disputed, contradicted, or resolved.
Conflicting reports remain separate evidence and corroboration records. A later
confirmation, contradiction, reopening, ceasefire violation, sanctions
amendment, or resolution appends a new event version; it never edits the prior
historical belief.

Raw text produces a `GeopoliticalEventCandidate`. The deterministic extractor
recognizes only reviewed phrases for explicit closures/reopenings,
sanctions/restrictions, transport or energy disruption, mobilization, named
actors, locations, and timezone-aware timestamps. It does not infer intent,
casualties, conflict outcomes, escalation probability, or asset direction.
Promotion requires a separate review record.

## Event versus mechanism

Events never contain a market conclusion. Separately provenanced transmission
paths link an event to candidate mechanisms over immediate, short, medium, or
long horizons. Different horizons can coexist with different confidence and
uncertainty. The reviewed Israel registry covers tourism/airlines,
shipping/imports, construction labor, defense spending, energy, sanctions,
FX/risk premium, capital flow, and government borrowing mechanisms.

These paths state possible economic transmission only. They are not predictions,
recommendations, allocations, or permission to execute.

## Corroboration

Corroboration distinguishes independent sources, syndicated copies, official
confirmation, official contradiction, and unresolved conflict. Matching source
identity or normalized fingerprint is classified as syndication, not independent
support. Corroboration becomes visible only at its local observation time.

## Research and replay

Research contexts show event status, confirmation, confidence, candidate paths,
contradictions, and source provenance explicitly. Replay snapshots include only
event versions, paths, and corroboration records visible at cutoff `T`. Later
casualty or outcome knowledge, confirmation, contradiction, or resolution cannot
leak backward.

The baseline reports event presence, confirmed count, unique mechanism exposure,
duration buckets, and a confirmation-weighted descriptive score. These are not
trading signals.

## Moral and safety boundary

Human harm is not reduced to a market opportunity. The lab does not forecast
casualties, optimize around conflict, infer political intent, or recommend trades.
Descriptions should remain factual, uncertainty-aware, and limited to reviewed
economic-transmission questions.

## Source and data limitations

Official Israeli/foreign statements, UN, ICAO, IMO, and IEA definitions are
registered but disabled because stable machine-readable revision, licensing, or
historical-visibility contracts have not been approved. BBC Business remains an
enabled established-news feed but is untrusted evidence and cannot directly
create a canonical event. No aviation, shipping, or energy indicator feed is
enabled in v0.12.
